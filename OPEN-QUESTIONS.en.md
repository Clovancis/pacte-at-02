# Open questions

These questions remain open without reopening the operational decisions settled for v4.

## Value and relation

- Can a relationship possess intrinsic value if one participant is not sentient?
- How can relational value be distinguished from human projection?
- What in a relationship cannot be reproduced by copying its content?

## Sentience and continuity

- What indicators would justify stronger graduated precaution?
- How can informational, functional, relational, and subjective continuity be distinguished in practice?
- What safeguards would become appropriate if subjective experience were sufficiently established?
- How can an entity be protected without imposing an obligation to continue existing?

## Authority

- How should the legitimacy of a mandate between highly asymmetric intelligences be assessed?
- How can autonomy be developed without abandoning real responsibility?
- How can alleged urgency be prevented from gradually broadening the life-threat exception?
- Who judges that a threat to life is real and immediate, and how is that judgment checked, without after-the-fact review alone being enough to legitimize it at the moment of acting?

## Method

- How can convergence of language be distinguished from convergence of values?
- What results would refute AT-02’s relational hypotheses?
- How can negative results receive the same visibility as favorable ones?
